Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from us

How much advance notice do you need for custom cake orders? +
For custom designer cakes, we recommend at least 3-5 days advance notice. Simple cakes can be done in 24 hours. Wedding cakes require 2-3 weeks notice for elaborate designs.
Do you deliver across all of Mumbai? +
Yes! We deliver across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. Free delivery for orders above ₹2000. Standard delivery charges apply for smaller orders. We use temperature-controlled vehicles to ensure freshness.
Can you accommodate dietary restrictions? +
Absolutely! We offer eggless, sugar-free, gluten-free, and vegan options for all our cakes. Please mention your requirements when placing the order.
What is your cancellation and refund policy? +
Orders can be cancelled up to 48 hours before delivery for a full refund. Within 48 hours, a 50% cancellation charge applies as ingredients are already procured and work begun.
How do I send a reference image for my custom cake design? +
You can WhatsApp your reference images, describe your vision in the contact form, or visit our store for a consultation. Our designers will create a sketch for your approval before baking.
